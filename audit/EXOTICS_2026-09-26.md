# Exotic medicine expansion — 2026-09-26

Adds 26 source-grounded protocols (161 total) for companion birds, rabbits, rodents, ferrets and reptiles. This expands retrieval/context and safety rules, not model weights.

## Coverage

- Birds: care/nutrition/prevention, respiratory distress, retained eggs, psittacosis, toxins/PTFE/metals, laboratory and medication limitations.
- Rabbits: care/prevention, anorexia/stasis versus obstruction, head tilt/E. cuniculi, route-specific antibiotic and fipronil restrictions.
- Rodents: distinct care considerations; guinea-pig anorexia/dental/vitamin C issues and antimicrobial risks; rat/mouse respiratory disease; hamster diarrhoea; chinchilla dental disease. Degu and gerbil support is explicitly limited to general safety and history, not specialist treatment protocols.
- Ferrets: nutrition/prevention, hypoglycaemia/insulinoma, GI foreign bodies, adrenal disease and urinary obstruction.
- Reptiles: species-specific microclimate/UVB/nutrition, metabolic bone disease, anorexia, respiratory and skin/shedding conditions, chelonian ivermectin restrictions.

## Integration

Species filtering prevents dog/cat protocols and lab panels from supplying exotic cases. Drug restrictions and care guidance remain present when symptom retrieval occupies top-k results. Concrete emergency rules bypass the Telegram interview/diagnostic branch; all other symptoms still require clinical assessment by the model. Negated symptoms and general reference questions are not classified as current emergencies by those rules.

Web chat, document interpretation, the legacy web helper and Telegram use shared species rules. Follow-up web questions retain an explicitly referenced pet within that conversation; clearing the conversation resets that association. Web and Telegram pet forms now accept the additional groups and preserve small weights. Telegram prevention shortcuts avoid the dog/cat vaccine/parasite tables for exotics.

New source links and access status are stored in each protocol and medical_evidence.json. Text is a short clinical synthesis, not reproduction of source chapters. No numerical drug dosing schedules were added. Full clinical validation is not claimed.

## Limits

The automated tests mock provider calls. They do **not** evaluate generated clinical answers from a live model, nor prove comprehensive clinical correctness. The Railway connector exposes variable names but redacts OPENAI_API_KEY; no live model evaluation was possible with that access. No patient messages were sent through production and no real user credits were consumed by the local tests. Drug safeguards are supplied as mandatory model instructions, not a post-generation drug-dose validator. Unknown formulations, doses and diseases still require species-specific evidence and clinician review.

Deployment status will be reported separately after the release is observed.
